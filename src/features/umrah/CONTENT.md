# Umrah guide content provenance

- **Duas:** Hisn al-Muslim entries from the adhkar snapshot (`src/features/adhkar/adhkarContent.json`),
  played from hisnmuslim.com's recordings: travel (206–208, 211–217), the mosque (20), the talbiya
  (233), the Black Stone (234), between the Yemeni corner and the Stone (235), Safa and Marwa (236),
  and visiting graves (165).
- **Evidence:** `umrahEvidence.json`, generated by `node scripts/build-umrah.mjs` — hadiths from
  HadeethEnc with their grade and attribution (each checked to mention what it is cited for), and
  2:125 (from «وَٱتَّخِذُوا۟» to «مُصَلًّى») and 2:158 from quran.com's reader text.
- **Instructions** (`umrahGuide.ts`) are written here and kept to what the scholars agree on. Where
  common practice isn't established, the guide says so: no per-round duas in tawaf, no specific dua
  on first seeing the Kaaba, no special dua for Quba. Rulings with real disagreement (fidya details)
  are referred to scholars rather than decided.
- The two rak'ahs' surahs (al-Kafirun, al-Ikhlas) are from Jabir's hadith in Sahih Muslim, which
  HadeethEnc doesn't carry; the guide names its source in the text.

This documents provenance, not an independent review by a scholar.
