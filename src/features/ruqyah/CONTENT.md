# Ruqyah content provenance

`ruqyahContent.json` is generated by `node scripts/build-ruqyah.mjs`; edit the passage and hadith
lists in that script, never the JSON.

- **Verses:** quran.com's `text_uthmani` (Hafs), the encoding the Uthmanic Hafs font is drawn for
  (api.alquran.cloud's text drops the tatweel before a dagger alif, which the font then draws as a
  dotted circle). Audio is Mishary Alafasy's ayah-by-ayah recitation, by global ayah number.
- **Which verses:** passages the Sunnah names for protection or healing carry a `sunnah` note with
  its source (al-Fatiha, Ayat al-Kursi, the end of al-Baqarah, al-Ikhlas and the Mu'awwidhatayn).
  The others are the selection reciters customarily use; the page says so.
- **Hadiths:** text, grade and attribution from HadeethEnc's ruqyah category; each card links to
  its explanation there. The words to say are the quoted part of the hadith, cut by the markers
  in the script.

This documents provenance, not an independent review by a scholar.
