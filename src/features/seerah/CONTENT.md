# Seerah content provenance

`seerahEvidence.json` and `arabiaMap.json` are generated by `node scripts/build-seerah.mjs`. Never edit
them by hand: change the lists in that script and rebuild. `seerahContent.ts` is written by hand and
copied unchanged into the app (almanara-mobile `src/core/seerah/`), with both JSON files.

- **Narration** (`beats`, `summary`, `lesson`) is written in our own words, following the account in
  «الرحيق المختوم» (Safiur-Rahman al-Mubarakpuri). It is not quoted from the book, which is under
  copyright. Where the sources differ (the day of the birth, the year of the Isra), the timeline gives
  no date or age rather than picking one. Numbers the sira books give only roughly (the armies at
  Uhud, the Trench, Hunayn and Tabuk) are left out of the narration.
- **Evidence:** 75 hadiths from HadeethEnc with their grade and attribution, each checked by the build
  to mention what it is cited for. All are graded صحيح except one حسن: Ali and the flag at Khaybar
  (10956), which shows its grade. 22 verses come from quran.com's reader text, with the surah names
  from its chapter list. No verse or hadith is typed by hand, not even in the narration: a beat that
  quotes the Quran ends with «…قول الله تعالى:» and shows the verse card.
- **Events told from the sira books without an attached hadith,** because HadeethEnc doesn't carry one:
  - the rebuilding of the Kaaba and the placing of the Black Stone (Musnad Ahmad, Ibn Ishaq);
  - Maysarah and Khadijah's trade to Syria (Ibn Ishaq);
  - Ja'far before the Najashi (Musnad Ahmad, from Umm Salamah);
  - the Prophet's return from al-Taif to Makkah;
  - the second pledge at al-Aqabah.

  These are the first beats a reviewer should check.

- **Map:** Natural Earth's 1:10m land polygons (public domain), clipped to 12°–34°N, 32°–52°E and
  simplified. Places are at their modern coordinates. Hunayn's valley is approximate.
- **Routes:** they trace the stations the sources name (the Hijrah's coastal way past Usfan, Qudayd
  and al-Kharrar; the caravan road north by al-Hijr to Tabuk), not surveyed tracks. A chapter marks
  them «المسار تقريبي». The crossing to Abyssinia is the only route at sea, listed in `AT_SEA`; every
  other waypoint is checked to be on land.
- **Pictures:** every beat off the map has a scene of its own, composed in `seerahContent.ts`
  from the parts in `SeerahScenes.tsx`.
  - **People** are faceless silhouettes of ordinary crowds only: the people of Makkah, pilgrims,
    armies, rows at prayer.
  - **The Prophet ﷺ, his family, and every companion a beat names are never drawn.** Where the
    story is about him, a light, his camel or a lit doorway stands in his place.
  - **Angels** are not drawn either; a light or a cloud stands for them.
  - **Tap notes** on the scenes are short facts drawn from the same sources as the narration. They
    are part of the review.
